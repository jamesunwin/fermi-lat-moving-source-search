# Data availability

The compact numerical release is stored in
`release/fl16y_v41_incident_e2_response_v1/`. It contains the candidate
catalogue, randomized-sky counts and inference, the complete 45-cell injection
response, physical-flux limits, position-dependent detection probabilities,
effective volumes, abundance translations, illustrative PBH calculations, and
publication figures.

The repository does not redistribute the full Fermi-LAT photon dataset, the
FL16Y catalogue, the energy-resolved exposure cube, or the per-trial injection
workspaces. These products are large or originate from public Fermi-LAT data
services. The release records the relevant analysis identity and input hashes;
the compact response tables are sufficient to reproduce the reported limits
and figures without rerunning the multi-day injection campaign.
